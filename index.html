AI Business Automation Platform ABAP

View repository↗

The problem

Small business processes often depend on disconnected tools, repetitive manual work, and automation that is difficult to monitor or recover. ABAP explores how those workflows can be managed securely from one platform.

What I built

A Docker-based platform bringing employee management, CRM, invoices, protected documents, scheduled workflows, background workers, signed integrations, provider-independent AI, and operational recovery into one system.

Inside ABAP — the case study ＋

Problem

Business records and repeatable processes become difficult to manage across disconnected tools.

Approach

CRM, employee management, invoicing, protected documents, workflow scheduling and execution tracking in one authenticated application.

Evidence & scope

A deployed application supported by synthetic-data demonstrations, isolated tests of signed n8n integration, and documented backup and recovery checks. This case study presents application capabilities and test evidence rather than commercial client results.

Authenticated browser→FastAPI + business services→PostgreSQLBackground worker→Signed webhooks→n8n

Architecture overview; it does not imply an active production n8n connection. The Advanced AI Assistant offers selectable models and safe synthetic demonstrations. It does not provide unrestricted free-form chat, live business-record access, or AI-initiated business actions.

Operational foundations: HTTPS, access controls, monitoring, encrypted backups, and verified recovery procedures.
